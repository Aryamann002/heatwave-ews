METHODOLOGY & PIPELINE

How it works

Four stages, from open forecast data to a decision an officer can act on and audit.

01 Ingest

Hourly temperature, relative humidity, 10m wind speed, and surface solar radiation from the Open-Meteo 7-day forecast, one point per district.

Refresh: every 6 hours
02 Index

UTCI, estimated Liljegren WBGT, and NOAA Heat Index computed rigorously at the hottest hour of each Indian Standard Time (IST) day.

Normals: ERA5 1991–2020
03 Warn

Two tracks evaluated: IMD departure-from-normal criteria and human thermal stress; the higher level is issued and every decision carries a full reasoning trace.

Issued: higher of the two tracks
04 Act

Draft advisories in English, Hindi and regional languages, officer approval, CAP 1.2 export, and resource allocation to the most exposed wards.

Dispatch: CAP 1.2 · SMS/email (simulated)
BIOPHYSICAL RATIONALE

Why two tracks?

Real case: Bhubaneswar, 28 May 2024 (ERA5 replay)

IMD criteria (Track 1)

GREEN

IMD heat-wave criteria use air temperature alone: a zone minimum plus the departure from the 1991–2020 normal, and how many days it persists.

  • Maximum air temperature 37.9°C
  • Departure from 1991–2020 normal +1.2°C (heat wave needs +4.5°C)
  • IMD criteria No heat wave
Blind spot: temperature-only criteria cannot see humidity, so humid coastal heat can be hazardous while the thermometer looks near normal.

Human thermal stress (Track 2)

ORANGE

UTCI models the body's heat balance from temperature, humidity, wind and radiation. Estimated WBGT and Heat Index are shown alongside.

  • Relative Humidity 58% at the hottest hour
  • Estimated WBGT 35.5°C
  • Universal Thermal Climate Index (UTCI) 40.5°C (very strong heat stress)
Same day: Heat Index 53.2°C. Humid air slows sweat evaporation, so the body sheds less heat at the same air temperature.
“Humid coastal heat can be dangerous before thermometers cross IMD thresholds. Heatwatch shows both and issues the higher.”

SAFEGUARDS

Built to be trusted, not just impressive.

An early warning only helps if officers can see why it was issued and who acted on it.

code

Deterministic, versioned alert rules

AI never sets an alert level. Levels come from versioned rules — IMD heat-wave criteria and the UTCI assessment scale — and each carries its reasoning trace. The LLM only translates advisories and parses questions.

verified_user

Human-in-the-loop signoff

Nothing is dispatched without officer or admin approval, and every approval, dispatch and task change is written to an audit log.

visibility

Honest limitations published openly

Shade-only UTCI, estimated WBGT, ERA5 smoothing of extremes and unvalidated Track 2 thresholds are all listed in the project's limitations register.
